When does AudioInteraction answer?

AudioInteraction-3B is a streaming voice model. It hears audio in 400 ms chunks and decides each time whether to speak or stay silent. We played the released model the same scenarios several times, changing only what fills the pause after each clip. Everything below is the exact audio it was fed and what it said back.

What we found (English, 26 questions per condition)

  1. With digital silence in the pauses it works: 23 of 26 questions answered, about 0.1 s after the question ends.
  2. Any real noise in the pause stops it. Room noise at -70 dBFS, which you can barely hear: 8/26. At -55 dBFS: 2/26. At -40 dBFS, an ordinary room: 0/26.
  3. Noise under everything, as the paper trains: 1/26.
  4. Their own web demo hides this. It replaces quiet mic input with zeros, so in a quiet room it answers 19/26. With noise loud enough to get through, 4/26.
  5. When it does speak, it also answers things not meant for it: 60% of fillers ("wah, so hot") and 59% of other people's conversations.
  6. In Hokkien it answers 2/26 even with silent pauses, and the replies have nothing to do with the question.

After fine-tuning on Hokkien (v2d, Hokkien, 26 questions per condition)

v2d is the released model fine-tuned on Singapore Hokkien, audio encoder included (2026-10-09). Same eval audio as above. Each scenario starts with the released model in digital silence for contrast.

  1. It now answers Hokkien questions: 19 of 26 with digital silence in the pauses (the released model in English: 23/26; in Hokkien: 2/26), about 0.2 s after the question ends.
  2. Unlike the released model, room noise doesn't stop it: 22/26 at -55 dBFS and 19/26 with noise under everything.
  3. The replies are on topic, but often wrong. Forced to reply at the end of each of the 24 eval questions, 6 replies answer correctly, 15 are on topic but wrong, 3 are off topic (released model in English: 10 / 12 / 2; before this round of tuning: 0 / 7 / 17). The mistakes are mostly numbers and facts.
  4. It keeps the released model's habits: it jumps in at a pause in the middle of a question (9/10; English release 8/10) and answers other people's conversations (76%; English release 59%).
  5. On 493 unseen questions of the training kind, it answers 54% correctly when spoken and 63% when typed.

Hokkien replies have a machine-translated English line under them. Same timeline key as above.
