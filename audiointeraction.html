When does AudioInteraction answer, and is it right?

AudioInteraction-3B is a streaming voice model. It hears audio in 400 ms chunks and decides each time whether to speak or stay silent. Below: the released model in English (the baseline), then the same model fine-tuned on Singapore Hokkien (v2d). Each one gets two tests.

  1. Online (streaming): 26 eval questions inside longer audio, with digital silence in the pauses. The model decides by itself when to speak. Does it reply right after the question, and is the reply right?
  2. Offline (forced reply): 24 of those questions, with the reply forced at the end of each question. It doesn't choose when to speak, so this shows only what it says.
